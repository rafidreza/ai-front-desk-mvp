### Product Images
No product images on the v1 public surface. Brand guidance explicitly asks for visual silence: no people, no screenshots, no rendered brains, no stock technology images.

## Palette

- Bone: `#EBEBEB`
- Ash: `#8A9499`
- Ash Dim: `#5A6166`
- Signal: `#54E3F9`

## Type

- Display: Space Grotesk if locally available, then Sora, Avenir Next, system sans.
- Body: Inter if locally available, then system sans.
- Mono: JetBrains Mono if locally available, then SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace.

## Voice

Restrained, confident, inevitable. Daemion announces. It does not sell.

Locked lines:
- Intelligence Beyond Instruction.
- Daemion builds cognition systems for the next industrial civilization.
- Not chatbots. Not apps. Cognition infrastructure.
- Daemion operates 6 divisions, 4 products, and 4 internal research labs.

## Design Signature

- Full black and graphite surfaces.
- Very few colors.
- Thin architectural dividers.
- Cyan used as signal, not decoration.
- Sparse motion: slow scan lines, subtle constellation grid, no bouncy interactions.

## Prohibited

- Purple gradients.
- Emoji.
- Stock people.
- Brain/circuit imagery.
- Friendly assistant/chatbot framing.
- Pricing, demo widgets, or self-serve signup language.
