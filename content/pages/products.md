## Section 2 — Eidolon {#eidolon}

**Eyebrow:** Tier — Consumer.

**Heading:** Eidolon.

**Positioning line:** A persistent cognitive counterpart.

**Body (two paragraphs):**
Eidolon is Daemion's consumer cognition surface — a persistent counterpart that reasons alongside its operator across years, contexts, and decisions. Not a productivity assistant. Not a generalized chatbot. A counterpart.

Eidolon holds long memory, models its operator's intent over extended horizons, and operates with autonomy bounded by explicit alignment. The operator retains directive authority; Eidolon retains continuity.

**Capabilities (single list, declarative — no bullet metaphors):**
- Persistent memory across years of operation
- Long-horizon goal tracking and intent modeling
- Reasoning under partial information
- Autonomy bounded by explicit alignment protocols
- Cross-device continuity through Daemion's substrate

**Availability:** Closed invitation. Direct application to the Eidolon research program through `eidolon@daemion.com`.

## Section 3 — Kheiron {#kheiron}

**Eyebrow:** Tier — Executive.

**Heading:** Kheiron.

**Positioning line:** Strategic intelligence for sovereign and executive systems.

**Body (two paragraphs):**
Kheiron is Daemion's executive-tier intelligence — built for the operators whose decisions shape institutional, governmental, and economic outcomes. Heads of state, sovereign system operators, and chief executives engaged in long-horizon strategic decisions.

Kheiron's surface is scenario reasoning, strategic simulation, and long-horizon planning under uncertainty. It does not advise. It models. The operator decides.

**Capabilities:**
- Scenario reasoning across multiple time horizons
- Strategic simulation of complex institutional systems
- Long-horizon planning under sustained uncertainty
- Geopolitical and economic modeling at sovereign scale
- Deployed under direct engagement only

**Availability:** Direct engagement only. No self-serve access. Inquiries via `kheiron@daemion.com`.

## Section 4 — Merqis {#merqis}

**Eyebrow:** Tier — Enterprise.

**Heading:** Merqis.

**Positioning line:** Operational intelligence at scale.

**Body (two paragraphs):**
Merqis is Daemion's enterprise intelligence operating system. It coordinates autonomous operations across the breadth of an institution — from customer interaction to internal coordination, from inventory to compliance, from inbound communication to outbound execution.

Merqis is not a workflow tool. It is the cognition layer that makes autonomous operations possible at the scale at which institutions actually operate.

**Capabilities:**
- Enterprise-wide operational coordination
- Autonomous operations across departments and functions
- Multi-channel cognition (customer-facing, internal, partner-facing)
- Long-cycle memory of institutional state
- Integration with existing enterprise infrastructure under Forge-hardened compute

**Availability:** Direct engagement with Daemion's enterprise office. Inquiries via `merqis@daemion.com`.

---

## Section 5 — Aevum {#aevum}

**Eyebrow:** Tier — Civilizational.

**Heading:** Aevum.

**Positioning line:** Deep-time intelligence.

**Body (two paragraphs):**
Aevum is Daemion's civilizational-tier modeling system. Long-horizon simulation of climate, economic systems, demographic dynamics, and the planetary infrastructure that supports them. Aevum is not built for quarterly cycles. It is built for the decades that institutional planning actually requires.

Aevum is operated under the Atlas division, with direct engagement by sovereign institutions, multilateral organizations, and selected research consortiums.

**Capabilities:**
- Climate and earth-systems modeling
- Long-cycle economic forecasting
- Demographic and migration simulation
- Planetary infrastructure intelligence
- Multi-decadal scenario reasoning

**Availability:** Sovereign and multilateral engagement only. Inquiries via `aevum@daemion.com`.
