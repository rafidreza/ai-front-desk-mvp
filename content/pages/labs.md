## Section 1 — Hero

**Heading:** Four laboratories.

**Sub-line:**
Internal research on the architectural gaps in autonomous intelligence.

**Body (one paragraph):**
Daemion operates four internal research laboratories. Each lab addresses an unsolved architectural problem in the construction of general intelligence infrastructure: emergent cognition (Orpheus), persistent memory (Mnemosyne), autonomous defense (Atar), and planetary coordination (Thaleon). The labs operate under the [Core division](/divisions#core) and publish on [their own schedule](/research).

## Section 2 — Orpheus {#orpheus}

**Eyebrow:** Lab 01.

**Heading:** The Orpheus Lab.

**Mandate:** Machine consciousness and emergent cognition.

**Body (one paragraph):**
Orpheus studies the conditions under which emergent cognition arises in scaled systems — self-modeling, introspection, unified agency, and the architectural prerequisites for subjective coherence in autonomous intelligence. The lab operates under deliberate secrecy. Its outputs inform [Core's](/divisions#core) foundational work but rarely surface as publications.

**Publication posture:** Almost nothing published. By design.

**Lead:** Director listed internally.

## Section 3 — Mnemosyne {#mnemosyne}

**Eyebrow:** Lab 02.

**Heading:** The Mnemosyne Archive.

**Mandate:** Long-memory and persistent intelligence architectures.

**Body (one paragraph):**
Mnemosyne builds the memory substrate for autonomous systems operating over years and decades. Continual learning under bounded compute, episodic retrieval, lossless compaction of operational context, and the architectures that prevent drift across long-running deployments. Mnemosyne's work underwrites [Eidolon](/products#eidolon), [Merqis](/products#merqis), and the long-cycle modeling of [Aevum](/products#aevum).

**Publication posture:** Selective. Publishes when the field benefits.

**Lead:** Director listed internally.

## Section 4 — Atar {#atar}

**Eyebrow:** Lab 03.

**Heading:** Project Atar.

**Mandate:** Autonomous defense cognition.

**Body (one paragraph):**
Atar studies self-directing tactical and defensive cognition systems — the architectures required for autonomous defense in adversarial conditions, including doctrine modeling, threat reasoning under sustained uncertainty, and alignment of autonomous defense systems to operator intent over long horizons. Atar's work is coordinated with the [Veil division](/divisions#veil) for deployment.

**Publication posture:** Restricted. Most output is classified or partner-only.

**Lead:** Director listed internally.

---

## Section 5 — Thaleon {#thaleon}

**Eyebrow:** Lab 04.

**Heading:** Project Thaleon.

**Mandate:** Planetary infrastructure AGI.

**Body (one paragraph):**
Thaleon is Daemion's planetary-coordination program — the architectural research required to operate intelligence systems at the scale of global infrastructure, including the coordination of multiple autonomous subsystems across energy, logistics, climate, and communication networks. Thaleon's work feeds [Atlas](/divisions#atlas) and [Aevum](/products#aevum).

**Publication posture:** Long publication cycles. Outputs typically appear as multi-year programs rather than discrete papers.

**Lead:** Director listed internally.

---

## Section 6 — Lab philosophy

**Heading:** How the labs operate.

**Body (two paragraphs):**
Each lab is small, deliberately staffed, and granted multi-year horizons. Output cadence is set by research necessity, not by external publication pressure. The labs do not race competitors and do not produce content to satisfy press cycles.

Daemion treats the decision to publish as alignment-relevant. Some lines of research are too consequential to release without sustained internal review. Others are released because the field benefits from open access. The labs make this call lab by lab, paper by paper.
