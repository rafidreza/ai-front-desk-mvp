## Section 2 — Hiring philosophy

**Heading:** How Daemion hires.

**Body (four short principles, each heading + one paragraph):**

### Architecture over enthusiasm

Daemion hires people whose work demonstrates architectural thinking — systems, abstractions, long-horizon program design. Enthusiasm for "AI" is not a qualification. Demonstrated capacity to build durable infrastructure is.

### Depth over breadth

We hire for depth in one or two domains, not surface familiarity with many. A research scientist with five years on memory architectures matters more than a generalist with a survey-level view of the field.

### Operating in restraint

Daemion's surfaces are quiet. The hiring process is the same. We do not run promotional campaigns at conferences. We do not gamify recruiting. The interview process is rigorous, structured, and respectful of the candidate's time.

### Hiring once, retaining long

Daemion is built for multi-year retention. Compensation, equity, work environment, and research autonomy are calibrated to that horizon. Annual attrition is treated as an architectural defect.

## Section 6 — Process

**Heading:** Hiring process.

**Body intro (one line):**
The process is consistent across roles, calibrated to depth.

**Process steps (numbered, one line each):**

1. Application review by the relevant domain lead.
2. First conversation — 45 minutes, structured. Domain depth, motivation, and fit.
3. Technical or research assessment — calibrated to the role. Most assessments are take-home, multi-day, and modeled on actual Daemion work. Compensation is provided for take-home work.
4. Panel — 2-4 conversations across the team you would join, plus one cross-division conversation.
5. Founder conversation — final-round only, for all senior roles and many earlier-career roles.
6. Decision and offer within 5 business days of the founder conversation.

**Total cycle:** typically 3-5 weeks from application to decision.

## Section 7 — Compensation and environment

**Heading:** Compensation.

**Body (two paragraphs):**

Compensation is competitive against frontier research and infrastructure laboratories — calibrated globally, paid in local currency or USD per role and location. Equity is meaningful and structured for long-term retention.

Work environment is Dhaka-first, in-office preferred. Limited remote capacity exists for senior research roles where the work justifies it. Daemion does not run a fully-remote organization; the cognitive coherence of long-cycle programs requires sustained in-person collaboration.
