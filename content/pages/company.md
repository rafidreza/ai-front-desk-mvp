## Section 2 — Mission

**Body (two short paragraphs):**

Daemion exists to build the cognition layer for institutions that operate on the order of decades. We do not pursue convenience, consumer mass-market, or short-horizon optimization. We pursue the architecture of autonomous intelligence as civilizational infrastructure.

The mission is multi-generational. The work is structured accordingly.

## Section 3 — Principles

**Heading:** Principles.

**Body intro (one line):**
Four principles shape how Daemion is built and operated.

**Principle list (four items, each heading + one paragraph):**

### Architecture over feature

Daemion organizes its work as architecture, not as products in isolation. Divisions, products, and labs share a common cognition substrate. New work earns its place in the architecture; it does not exist as a sales line item.

### Restraint over expression

Daemion's surfaces are silent. Our published outputs are sparse and deliberate. Restraint is a design discipline, not a marketing aesthetic.

### Alignment as architecture

Alignment is treated as a property of system design, not a layer added at deployment. Every autonomous system Daemion builds is constructed under explicit alignment constraints from the architectural level upward.

### Long horizons

Daemion operates on multi-year and multi-decade horizons. Quarterly cycles do not govern research direction, product cadence, or hiring decisions.
