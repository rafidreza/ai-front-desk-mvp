**Body intro (one line):**
Inquiries are routed by office. Use the address that fits the engagement.

General inquiries, partnerships at the company level, executive engagement, press, and any inquiry that does not fit a more specific office.

## Section 4 — Response posture

**Heading:** What to expect.

**Body (one paragraph):**
Routine inquiries are acknowledged within two business days. Substantive responses follow on a cadence appropriate to the inquiry — same week for partnerships, longer for research collaborations and sovereign engagements requiring internal coordination.

Daemion does not auto-respond. Every reply is composed by a person at the relevant office.

---

## Section 5 — What Daemion does not handle

**Heading:** Out of scope.

**Body (single paragraph):**
Daemion does not respond to unsolicited sales outreach, conference-speaker requests outside the press office, marketing partnership pitches, or recruiter inquiries unrelated to active candidates. Submissions of this kind are not acknowledged and are filtered at the inbox layer.
