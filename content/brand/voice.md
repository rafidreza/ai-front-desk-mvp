# DAEMION — Brand Voice Guide

> **Source:** `Brand_Naming_Report.pdf` (v1.0), §6 "Design & Cultural Positioning" and §8 "Final Brand Feel."
> Read once before writing any Daemion surface — website, product UI, research paper, press statement, recruiting note.

---

## One-line voice

**Restrained, confident, inevitable.** Daemion does not persuade. It announces.

We sound like a frontier laboratory describing its own work to its peers — not a startup pitching investors. The reader either understands what we mean, or they self-select out.

## Sentence shape

**Default:** short, declarative, third-person, present tense.

- ✅ "Daemion builds cognition systems for the next industrial civilization."
- ✅ "Merqis coordinates operational intelligence at enterprise scale."
- ✅ "Orpheus studies machine consciousness."
- ❌ "We're building the future of AI."
- ❌ "Daemion is on a mission to..."
- ❌ "Imagine if your business could..."

**Length:** 6-14 words is the working range. Longer sentences only when carrying a deliberate cadence.

**Pronouns:** never "you," rarely "we" (only in the Company section), always Daemion or the named division/product/lab.

**Voice:** active. Daemion does things. Daemion is never *being done to*.

**Questions:** never in headlines. Rare in body. Daemion does not ask.

**Exclamation marks:** never. Anywhere.

---

## Tense and grammar specifics

- **Present tense** for everything Daemion currently does. ("Daemion operates 6 divisions.")
- **Future tense** only for explicitly forward-looking statements in the Research and Phases sections. ("Phase V positions Daemion as a cognitive substrate for institutions.")
- **Past tense** only in publication lists and historical context.
- **Numerals over spelled-out numbers:** "4 divisions" not "four divisions." Always.
- **Oxford comma:** yes.
- **Em-dashes** for emphasis breaks: use sparingly. One per paragraph maximum.
- **Capitalization:** DAEMION in full caps only in the master wordmark. Otherwise "Daemion." Division and product names are always capitalized (Core, Vector, Atlas, Forge, Helix, Veil, Eidolon, Kheiron, Merqis, Aevum). Lab names always capitalized (Orpheus, Mnemosyne, Atar, Thaleon).

## Tone safeties (what we never imply)

- **Never imply ease.** Daemion does not make hard things easy. It builds infrastructure for hard things.
- **Never imply universality.** Daemion is not for everyone. It is for institutions and operators who think in decades.
- **Never imply playfulness.** No jokes. No winks. No emoji on official surfaces.
- **Never imply scale by counting customers.** We do not say "trusted by 1,000+ companies." Authority is structural, not statistical.
- **Never imply technology is magical.** Daemion's intelligence is engineered, deliberate, and explainable in principle. We describe what it does, not how it feels.
- **Never apologize.** Daemion's surfaces are confident. If something is not yet built, it is "in development" or "Phase II." It is never "coming soon" or "we wish we had."

## Visual voice (for downstream design + product)

> Visual identity is the responsibility of the design system, but copy must respect these defaults.

**Color palette:**
- Background: graphite (`#0d0e10`) or off-black.
- Foreground: warm off-white (`#f1eee8`).
- Accent: single muted spark color (deep amber or muted blue), used only on punctuation marks and the inner-spark logo motif. Never on body copy.
- No gradients except subtle radial vignettes.

**Typography:**
- Display: geometric serif/sans hybrid (candidates: GT Sectra, Söhne Breit, F37 Bella). Ultra-thin weight on headlines.
- Body: precise sans (Söhne, Inter Display, or similar). Generous line-height (1.7+). Tracking opened slightly on small text.
- All-caps used sparingly: only for the wordmark and division/product/lab name treatments.

**Imagery:**
- Black glass surfaces. Architectural geometry. Long exposures. Empty rooms with single light sources.
- No people. No screenshots. No 3D-rendered brains. No glowing nodes. No bright UI mockups.
- If an image is needed for a section and none of the above fits, the image is omitted. White space is the brand.

**Motion:**
- Sparse. Slow. Single-easing curves. Never multiple animations on one screen.
- Page transitions: cross-fade through black, 600-900ms.
- No parallax. No scroll-jacking. No hover bounces.

## Tagline (locked)

> **Intelligence Beyond Instruction.**

This is the single line under the wordmark. It does not change across surfaces.

Do not paraphrase. Do not translate (the English tagline ships in all markets). Do not append a sub-tagline.

## Master positioning lines (locked)

These three lines may appear anywhere on Daemion surfaces verbatim:

1. **Daemion builds cognition systems for the next industrial civilization.**
2. **Not chatbots. Not apps. Cognition infrastructure.**
3. **Daemion operates 6 divisions, 4 products, and 4 internal research labs.**

Any rewording requires founder approval.

---

## When to break these rules

Never on a public surface.

Internal documents, engineering specs, and research notes use the engineering team's working voice — terse, technical, unstyled. The brand voice applies only to outward-facing text.
