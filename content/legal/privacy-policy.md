## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Daemion processes personal data submitted through:

- The public website at `daemion.com`.
- Inquiry forms and mailboxes listed on `/contact`.
- Careers applications submitted to `careers@daemion.com` or through posted role surfaces.
- Direct correspondence with any Daemion office.

Personal data processed by Daemion's deployed products (Eidolon, Kheiron, Merqis, Aevum) is governed by separate engagement-specific data processing agreements, executed at the point of direct engagement. Those agreements supersede this Policy for the data they describe.

## 3. What data Daemion collects

Daemion collects only the data needed to respond to inquiries, evaluate careers applications, and operate the public website.

| Category | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Name, organization, role | Inquiry form, direct email | Route the inquiry to the correct office and respond |
| Email address | Inquiry form, direct email, careers application | Reply to the inquiry; correspond on the application |
| Inquiry content | Inquiry form, direct email | Read and respond |
| Careers application materials (CV, portfolio, supporting documents) | Careers submission | Evaluate fit for open or standing-interest roles |
| Interview notes and assessment results | Hiring process | Evaluate the candidate; retain only with the candidate's consent |
| IP address, user agent, request timing | Web server logs | Operate the website; detect abuse |
| Page-view paths (aggregated, no cookies) | Server-side log analysis | Understand which pages are read; inform editorial decisions |

Daemion **does not** deploy third-party analytics, advertising trackers, social-media pixels, or session-replay tools on the public website. Page-view analysis is server-side and aggregated.

Daemion **does not** set cookies for analytics, marketing, or personalization on the public website. Strictly-necessary cookies for the inquiry form's anti-spam mechanism are set only when the form is loaded; they are session-only.

## 4. Why Daemion processes the data

Daemion processes the data described in §3 for the following purposes:

1. Respond to inquiries received through the website or direct email.
2. Evaluate and respond to careers applications.
3. Operate and secure the public website.
4. Comply with legal obligations (tax records, regulatory disclosures, lawful requests).
5. Maintain institutional records of correspondence with sovereign, enterprise, research, and capital partners, where retention is appropriate to the relationship.

Daemion does not use website-visitor data, inquiry data, or careers data for marketing, profiling, advertising, or training of any Daemion product.

## 5. Legal basis

The legal basis for processing under the Bangladesh PDP framework and equivalent international standards is:

- **Inquiry data:** legitimate interest of the inquirer in receiving a response, and Daemion's legitimate interest in operating an institutional correspondence channel.
- **Careers data:** the candidate's consent and the legitimate interest of evaluating fit for the role.
- **Website log data:** legitimate interest in operating and securing the site.
- **Records of institutional correspondence:** legitimate interest in maintaining institutional records.

## 9. Security

Daemion maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data processed:

- Correspondence systems require multi-factor authentication.
- Inquiry-form submissions are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest.
- Careers application materials are accessible only to the hiring panel for the relevant role.
- Server logs are accessible only to a restricted infrastructure team.
- Backups are encrypted and access-controlled.
- The Company office reviews access logs on a periodic basis.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident is reasonably likely to affect a person whose data Daemion processes, Daemion notifies the affected parties within 72 hours of becoming aware of the incident.

Vulnerability reports are accepted at `security@daemion.com`. A PGP key is published at [URL TBD].

## 10. Individual rights

If Daemion processes your personal data, you have the right to:

1. **Access** — receive a copy of the data Daemion holds about you.
2. **Correct** — request correction of inaccurate data.
3. **Delete** — request deletion, subject to legal retention obligations described in §7.
4. **Restrict** — request restriction of processing.
5. **Object** — object to processing on legitimate-interest grounds.
6. **Withdraw consent** — where processing relies on consent (e.g., extended careers retention).
7. **Lodge a complaint** — with the Bangladesh data protection authority once the PDP regulator is operational.

To exercise any right, contact `privacy@daemion.com`. Daemion acknowledges every request within 3 business days and responds substantively within 30 days. Where a request requires longer review, Daemion communicates the timeline in writing.

For data-deletion requests specifically, see [Data Deletion](/data-deletion).

## 11. Minors

Daemion's public surface is not directed at minors. Daemion does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If material submitted to Daemion indicates that the submitter is a minor, the submission is deleted and the submitter is informed where contact is feasible.
