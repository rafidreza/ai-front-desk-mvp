## 1. Scope

This page describes how to request deletion of personal data Daemion processes through:

- Inquiry forms on `daemion.com`.
- Direct correspondence with any Daemion mailbox.
- Careers applications.

For data processed by Daemion's deployed products (Eidolon, Kheiron, Merqis, Aevum) under direct engagement, deletion paths and response timelines are defined in the engagement-specific data processing agreement. Contact `privacy@daemion.com` if you are uncertain which path applies to you.

## 2. What deletion covers

A deletion request, once verified and processed, removes the following data Daemion holds about you:

| Source | Data deleted |
|---|---|
| Inquiry form submissions | The full submission record, including name, organization, email, role, and inquiry content. |
| Direct email correspondence | Email threads in Daemion's correspondence systems, except where retention is required by law or by an active institutional matter (see §6). |
| Careers application | Application materials (CV, portfolio, supporting documents), interview notes, and assessment results — except where you have given written consent for continued retention. |

## 3. How to request deletion

Send an email from the address associated with the data you want deleted to:

**`privacy@daemion.com`**

Include in the request:
- The category of data (inquiry, correspondence, careers application, or all three).
- A brief description of the data — date of submission, role you applied to, or office you corresponded with — to help Daemion locate the records.
- Confirmation that you are the person whose data is being deleted, or that you have authority to act on their behalf.

If sending email is not feasible, write to the postal address in §9.

## 4. Verification

Daemion may ask one verification question to confirm the request is from you — for example, the approximate date of your submission, the role you applied to, or an alternate email address you used. This is to protect against deletion requests submitted by third parties impersonating the data subject.

Verification does not require identity documents in most cases. Where it does (rare), Daemion describes the standard in writing and processes the documents under restricted access.

## 5. Response timeline

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgment of request | Within 3 business days |
| Verification questions (if any) | Within 5 business days |
| Substantive response and confirmation of deletion | Within 30 calendar days of verified request |

Where the request cannot be completed within 30 days — for example, where archived backups must be rotated before deletion is complete — Daemion communicates the revised timeline in writing.

## 6. When deletion may be delayed or partially withheld

Daemion may retain certain data despite a deletion request, only where retention is required by law or by an active matter. Specifically:

| Reason | Examples | Retention basis |
|---|---|---|
| Tax and financial records | Invoices, payment receipts | Bangladesh tax law (currently 7 years) |
| Active legal or regulatory matter | Ongoing dispute, lawful request from a competent authority | Suspension of deletion until the matter is concluded |
| Active institutional matter | Live partnership negotiation or active contract discussion that depends on the correspondence | Retention until the matter is concluded; subject to the corresponding party's consent for continued retention |
| Anonymized aggregates | Web log statistics that cannot identify you | Retained indefinitely as non-personal data |

Where Daemion retains data under one of these exceptions, the retention basis and expected duration are described in the response to your deletion request.

## 7. Confirmation

When deletion is complete, Daemion sends a confirmation email describing:

- What was deleted.
- Anything retained under §6, with the basis and expected duration.
- The contact for follow-up if you believe the response is incomplete.

## 8. Withdrawal of consent for extended retention

If you previously gave Daemion written consent for extended retention of careers materials (e.g., for future consideration), you may withdraw that consent at any time by writing to `privacy@daemion.com`. Daemion deletes the materials within 30 days of receiving the withdrawal.

If you believe Daemion has not handled your deletion request appropriately, you may:

1. Write to `privacy@daemion.com` with the subject line `Deletion complaint — [reference]`. The Company office reviews complaints within 10 business days.
2. Lodge a complaint with the Bangladesh data protection authority once the PDP regulator is operational.
